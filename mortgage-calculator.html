How lenders decide

Every federally regulated lender has to “stress test” you at the higher of your rate + 2% or 5.25%. At that test rate:

  • GDS (mortgage, property tax, heat and half of condo fees) should be no more than 39% of your gross income.
  • TDS (all of the above plus your other debts) should be no more than 44%.

This is an estimate. Your actual approval depends on your credit, employment and the lender. A pre-approval gives you the real number, and I can connect you with a mortgage professional.

Closing cost breakdown

These calculators give estimates for planning only and are not a mortgage approval or financial advice. Payments assume a fixed rate compounded semi-annually, as Canadian law requires for fixed-rate mortgages. CMHC premiums are 2.80% to 4.00% of the mortgage depending on your down payment, plus 0.20% for a 30-year insured amortization; Ontario charges 8% PST on the premium at closing.
